🚀 Excited to share our Final-Year B.Tech Capstone Project at SR University, School of Computer Science and Artificial Intelligence!

Title: CNC Tool Wear Detection in CNC Milling Operations Using Machine Learning ⚙️🤖

In precision subtractive manufacturing, cutting with a dull tool ruins expensive aerospace/automotive parts and causes thousands of dollars in spindle damage. But external sensors cost over $20,000 and get destroyed by metal chips and coolant.

Our team developed a completely sensorless, Explainable AI system that monitors cutting tool health by tapping into the CNC controller's existing internal drive signals (motor currents and axis positions) with ZERO extra hardware costs!

💡 Key Project Highlights:
✅ University of Michigan SMART Lab Dataset: 18 CNC milling experiments across 48 synchronized channels at 10 Hz.
✅ 425-Feature Multi-Domain Pipeline: 9 statistical moments computed across 47 channels with unbiased sample variance (ddof=1) + operating feed rate and clamping pressure.
✅ Rigorous LOOCV Benchmark: Evaluated across 7 ML models using Leave-One-Out Cross-Validation.
✅ Champion Decision Tree Model: Achieved 77.78% Accuracy, 81.82% F1-Score, and a vital 90.00% Recall (catching 9 out of 10 worn tools)!
✅ Explainable AI (XAI): Transparent physical decision rules linked directly to Z-axis cutting thrust deflection and CNC controller block delays.
✅ Interactive Web Dashboard: Built a full-featured Streamlit application (app.py) for real-time shop-floor operator diagnostics.
